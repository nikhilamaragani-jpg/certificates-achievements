## Evidence principles

- A course, workshop, certificate, internship, offer letter and portfolio project are not interchangeable; each has its own status.
- The BE10X certificate copy has blank recipient and issue-date fields, so personal attribution and date need confirmation.
- The Summer of AI document is an **offer letter**, not proof of acceptance or completion.
- The Power BI workshop dashboards are workshop artifacts, not client work or independently verified business results.
- The claims-operations portfolio case study uses explicitly **synthetic** data.
