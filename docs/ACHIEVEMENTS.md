## How to read the evidence

- **Certificates** document the issuer's stated course, workshop, internship or training status; see the [credential register](CREDENTIALS.md).
- **Offer letters** are kept distinct from completion evidence.
- **Workshop artifacts** show practice and learning evidence, not professional/client delivery.
- **Portfolio projects** demonstrate documented practice; synthetic data is labeled wherever used.
- **Recorded achievements without a supporting file** are identified transparently and should not be described as independently verified.
